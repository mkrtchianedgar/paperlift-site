What PaperLift does

PaperLift is a workout journal for lifting. Open it, write down your sets, close it. There are no plans, no coaching and no feed. Just your workouts, on pages that look like a gym notebook.

Log fast

  • Add an exercise, type the weight and reps, tick the set. The next set is filled in from the last one.
  • Over 560 exercises, each with its own ink drawing, or make your own.
  • Barbell, dumbbell, machine, bodyweight and cardio (time and distance).
  • Weights in kg or lb, per exercise.
  • A rest timer that starts when you tick a set.

See what you did

  • History of every workout, by week and month.
  • Personal bests marked with a red stamp.
  • An estimated one-rep max (Est. 1RM) chart for each exercise.

On your iPhone

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity while you lift, and a Control Center button to start a workout.
  • Syncs with your own iCloud. There is no account to make.
  • Optional: save finished workouts to Apple Health. PaperLift only writes workouts there and never reads your Health data.
  • Share a workout card to your stories or messages. You choose what's on it.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. Export your workouts as CSV any time, or delete everything in Settings. Read the Privacy Policy.

Questions

What is PaperLift?

PaperLift is a lifting journal for iPhone. It looks and feels like a paper gym notebook: you write down each exercise, set, weight and reps, and the app adds up the numbers. It has no plans and no coaching.

Is PaperLift free?

Yes. Logging workouts is free with no limits. PaperLift Pro is optional and adds extra themes, app icons, Trends charts and monthly and yearly recaps for $19.99 a year or $49.99 once.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your workouts are saved on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud.

Does PaperLift collect my data?

No. PaperLift has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. You can export your workouts as a CSV file or delete everything in Settings.

Which exercises are included?

Over 560 exercises, each with its own ink drawing, covering barbell, dumbbell, machine, bodyweight and cardio. You can also make your own exercises.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you turn it on. PaperLift can save finished workouts to Apple Health. It only writes workouts there and never reads your Health data.

Can I use kilograms or pounds?

Both. You can type weights in kg or lb, and choose the unit per exercise.

PaperLift needs iOS 26 or later. More help on the Support page.
